# Visual review — JavaScript possum proposal

Reviewed locally in headless Edge on 22 September2026. This is a browser artwork study, not native APK evidence.

- Opened the original approved09 isolated image and current whole guide sprite before construction.
- Rendered and inspected the first drawing at420px, portrait390px viewport, a wide1200px page and the success pose with visible attachment points.
- First pass had too much contrast at the sleeves and an excessively compressed arm target. Revised overlapping curved contours, matched arm material to the torso and moved the success reach to an outward pose with a consistent elbow bend.
- Second review tucked the far eye into the head plane, clipped eyes against the intact face silhouette, softened the cheek edge and arm junction, and increased stable paper fibers. The stage mirrors the complete character so the muzzle faces the instruction, while the comparison drawing preserves09's original orientation.
- Re-rendered and inspected `drawing.png`, `review-portrait.png`, `joints-success.png`, `review-wide.png` and the36-frame `filmstrip.png`. Head and chest remain whole through blinks, the tail stays continuous, and the lower body is hidden only by the real canvas bottom. The camera preview control now triggers the camera cue rather than pretending to take a photograph.
- `render-check.json` reports no browser errors. The interactive controls include replay, pause, time scrub, reduced motion, joint diagnostics and a grounded seated study. The transparent `atlas.png` contains six whole320px cells in clip order, generated directly from the same source. It is an optional construction artifact, not a production asset replacement.

## Remaining artistic limitations

The drawing is smoother and simpler than09's hand-cut shapes. Its procedural texture is regular paper fleck/fiber variation, not the reference's larger handmade paper structure. The body is slightly more rounded and the wrist/paw gesture remains stylized. The speech pointer aims toward the transformed muzzle, but the surrounding phone scene is a composition study rather than a complete app layout. These differences are visible in the side-by-side reference. No claim of owner approval or exact reference equivalence is made.
