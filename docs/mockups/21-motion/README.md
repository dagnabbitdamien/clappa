# Current motion review

Open `http://127.0.0.1:17448/21-motion/index.html` while `tools/serve-design-review.mjs` is running, or open `index.html` locally. The review uses bundled media and has no external service dependency.

The board has full vertical travel with a damped body spring and a separate angular hinge response. It is never opacity-faded. The bar opens about the left screw, returns under gravity, contacts its stop and adds a small body impulse. Illustrative QR playback begins only after the board/bar are fixed. The three phone clacks use explicit preview onset times.

Nine motion scenarios, continuous and slowed playback, frame scrubbing, portrait/landscape, reduced motion, complete unmasked puppet and original-reference comparison. New paper master and nine true-alpha puppet sprites replace the rejected vector reconstruction. A concealed-body diagnostic defines the OBS attachment layout. There are nine MP4 review exports and a source/asset ZIP.

The installed APK/plugin are unchanged. These are actual running asset/motion prototypes, not a claim of final design approval or successful native integration. All image and QR data in the board are illustrative.
