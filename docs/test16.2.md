# Test16.2 — mirrored accent and proportioned hinge

Android 0.3.0-test16.2 (25) uses a 16dp menu band made from two mirrored 8dp rows. No menu hinge or metal plate. Capture-board art remains unchanged.

The OBS hinge is redrawn directly at its 51.2-unit height: a narrower metal plate, a pivot at (14,12.8), round fixing screws at (10,40) and (28,40), subtle metal shading and circular screw heads. No nonuniform scale is applied to the hinge. Animated composition renders at 2× resolution while maintaining the 872×480 source dimensions. Static QR pixels are enlarged with nearest-neighbour sampling, with no interpolated QR edges. QR timing, content and layout remain unchanged.

Update APK and OBS plugin together. This is a presentation test build; live Twitch and physical camera checks are not repeated.
